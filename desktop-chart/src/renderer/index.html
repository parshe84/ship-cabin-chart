ENC charts · S-57

Official vector charts from hydrographic offices, in the unencrypted S-57 format (.000 cells with their .001… updates, a folder such as ENC_ROOT, or a .zip). Free sources include NOAA for all US waters (charts.noaa.gov → ENC). Encrypted S-63 cells (C-MAP, Primar, AVCS) cannot be opened. Not for navigation in place of an approved ECDIS.
